The Problem

Why EcoLoop AI needs to exist

Millions of people in India have no simple, trusted way to buy affordably, sell what they don't need, or earn from waste.

💸

Affordability gap

Students and middle-class families can't always afford new products but have no trusted platform to buy quality second-hand items safely.

🗑️

Waste going to landfills

Reusable products and recyclable waste are thrown away daily because there's no easy way to sell or trade them.

🤖

No AI-powered ecosystem

Existing platforms like OLX only do buying and selling. Nobody combines affordability, resale, and recycling with AI intelligence in one place.
